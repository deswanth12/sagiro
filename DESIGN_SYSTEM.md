# PaisaPilot Official Brand Identity & Design System (PRD v3.5)

> **Tagline**: *"KNOW YOUR MONEY. LIVE WITH CONFIDENCE."*

This document specifies the official brand identity, logo geometry, color gradients, spatial depth rules, and copywriting guidelines for **PaisaPilot**.

---

## 🎨 Official Brand Identity & Logo Geometry

### 1. Logo Symbol Geometry
- **Primary Emblem**: Stylized 3D-gradient **'P'** with ascending financial growth bars embedded inside the inner counter space of the letter 'P'.
- **Symbolism**: Represents financial control, steady growth velocity, and piloting your money with confidence.

### 2. Official Color Palette & Gradients (aligned with `UI_UX.md`)
- **Canvas Background**: Deep Midnight Black (`#0B0F19`)
- **Surface**: Obsidian Container (`#141B2D`)
- **Card / Elevated Glass**: (`#1B2435`) with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` 1px stroke
- **Primary Brand Accent**: **Electric Mint (`#00F5A0`)** — used across buttons, progress, focus rings
- **Secondary Accent**: Electric Mint Glow (`#00C98A`)
- **Success**: (`#22C55E`)
- **Warning**: Amber (`#F59E0B`)
- **Danger**: Coral (`#FF6B6B`)
- **Text Primary**: White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Text Secondary**: Cool Grey (`#9CA3AF`)
- **Wordmark Typography**:
  - `Paisa`: Bold Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - `Pilot`: Electric Mint (`#00F5A0`)

### 3. Logo Variations & Applications
- **App Icon**: Darksquircle container featuring the 3D-gradient 'P' emblem.
- **Monochrome Icon**: High-contrast black 'P' emblem on crisp white rounded squircle.
- **Adaptive Icon**: Circular Android launcher icon variant with deep navy background (`#070B19`).
- **Wordmark Lockup**: Horizontal logo containing 'P' emblem + `PaisaPilot` wordmark + uppercase tagline (`KNOW YOUR MONEY. LIVE WITH CONFIDENCE.`).

## 📐 Spatial Grid & Layout Architecture

- **Base Grid**: 8pt grid system (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`).
- **Screen Margins**: `20px` horizontal padding on mobile.
- **Card Padding**: `18px` internal padding for primary cards.
- **Corner Radius Scale**:
  - `32px` — Floating Navigation Bar & Hero Sheets
  - `28px` — Hero Safe Today Fuel Gauge Card
  - `20px` — Standard Glass Cards & Dialogs
  - `14px` — Small Input Fields, Buttons & Chips
  - `8px` — Micro Badges & Indicator Lines
