- **PaisaPilot Free**: 100% On-device bank SMS parsing (50+ Indian banks), CSV statement import, manual expense entry, `Safe Today` daily spend limit, `Timeline`, and `Vault`.
- **PaisaPilot Pro** (₹99/mo, ₹499/yr, ₹999 Lifetime): Private Sync™ (E2EE Google Drive backup), automated background sync, unlimited Money Replay history, PDF statement import, advanced `Guide` insights, and premium widgets.
