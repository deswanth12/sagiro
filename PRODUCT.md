## 🔁 The 10 Habit Loops & Daily Engagement Anchors

1. **☀️ Money Weather**: Dynamic daily forecast status (*"Sunny ☀️ • Safe Spend: ₹430"* or *"Heavy Spending Ahead 🌧️"*).
2. **🔥 Money Streak**: Gamified streak tracker for staying within budget limits (`8 Days Within Budget 🔥`).
3. **🏆 Daily Win**: Positive reinforcement (*"You spent ₹120 less than your safe budget today!"*).
4. **🌱 No-Spend Day Tracker**: Visual calendar badges (*"12 No-Spend Days This Month"*).
5. **🎭 Spending Personality**: Behavioral persona matching (*The Planner 📐*, *Food Explorer 🍕*, *Weekend Spender 🥳*, *The Saver 💎*).
6. **🥇 Monthly Awards**: Interactive achievements (*Most Improved Saver*, *Food Budget Master*, *Subscription Hunter*).
7. **⭕ Budget Health Ring**: Visual Apple Fitness-style healthy budget indicator (`82%`).
8. **📅 Weekly Reflection**: Every Sunday summary with actionable habit tips.
9. **🎬 Annual Wrapped**: Spotify Wrapped style year-end financial recap.
10. **🎯 Daily Money Mission**: Light gamified daily challenge (*"Spend less than ₹400 today"*).

## 📈 Metric Thresholds Before AI Integration

Before introducing automated AI Money Coaching capabilities, PaisaPilot will focus on reaching:
- 🚀 **10,000 Total Downloads**
- 👥 **1,000 Daily Active Users (DAU)**
- 💎 **100 Premium Subscribers**
