# PaisaPilot Business Model & Go-to-Market Strategy (PRD v3.0)

> **"Know your money. Live with confidence."**

This document outlines the strategic business model, freemium tiering, trust guarantees, and user progression roadmap for **PaisaPilot**.

---

## 1. Monetization Architecture

### Tier 1: PaisaPilot Free (Core Experience)
- 100% On-Device SMS Auto-Parsing (50+ Indian Commercial Banks)
- Manual Transaction Logging & Category Tagging
- HDFC/Kotak/ICICI CSV Statement Import
- **Safe Today** Fuel Gauge Daily Spending Limit
- **Financial Timeline** Feed with Date Grouping & Search
- Basic Monthly Replay Summary

### Tier 2: PaisaPilot Pro (Paid Tier)
- **Pricing**: ₹99/month, ₹499/year (Recommended), or ₹999 Lifetime Access.
- **Features Unlocked**:
  - Unlimited Historical Money Replay & Annual Summaries
  - Advanced On-Device Financial Guide Insights (Powered by Money Brain™)
  - Bank PDF Statement OCR & Parsing
  - Multiple Custom Budgets (Personal, Business, Vacation)
  - Unlimited Savings Goals & Upcoming Bill Tracking
  - Encrypted Local SAF Backup & Restore Engine
  - Signature Financial Widgets & Custom Themes

---

## 2. Absolute Trust Directives (Non-Negotiables)

- ❌ **NO Loan Ads / Credit Card Spam**
- ❌ **NO Gambling or Predatory Financial Products**
- ❌ **NO Selling User Data or Third-Party Analytics Trackers**
- ❌ **NO Cloud Data Harvesting** — 0 Bytes uploaded to external servers.

---

## 3. The Retention & Revenue Growth Equation

$$\text{Downloads} \longrightarrow \text{Active Users} \longrightarrow \text{Retention} \longrightarrow \text{Trust} \longrightarrow \text{Pro Conversion} \longrightarrow \text{Sustainable Revenue}$$

- **Retention Goal**: Focus on the 30-Second Morning Ritual. If users check `Safe Today` every morning, retention and Pro conversion follow naturally.

---

## 4. Year 1 Go-to-Market Milestones

1. **1,000 Downloads**: Organic launch on Play Store, Product Hunt, Twitter/X, and Reddit (`r/IndiaInvestments`, `r/PersonalFinanceIndia`).
2. **100 Daily Active Users (DAUs)**: Habit formation around the morning check-in.
3. **50 Genuine Reviews (4.7★+ Rating)**: Prompting satisfied users after 7 consecutive days of usage.
4. **First 10 Paying Customers**: Validating the ₹499/year and ₹999 Lifetime value proposition.
5. **First 100 Paying Customers**: Establishing sustainable recurring revenue and reinvesting in product design.
